\MakeAbstract{
    双轮足机器人将轮式移动的高效性与腿式机构的地形适应性集于一身，但现有平台普遍存在结构复杂、执行器冗余和在线计算量大的问题，给小型化样机开发和嵌入式实时控制带来了困难。本文以混合式双轮足机器人为对象，研究其结构设计、动力学建模和轻量级控制方法，力求在较低的控制复杂度下实现稳定平衡、速度调节和腿长支撑。

    机械结构方面，完成了整机构型、闭环腿部机构、躯干承载结构、链传动和足端轮组的设计与关键执行器选型校核。建模方面，将闭环腿部机构抽象为可伸缩虚拟腿，建立了平面等效运动学与动力学模型，推导了面向 LQR 的线性状态空间表达式；同时，证明了本文混合式闭环腿部机构与标准五连杆机构在运动学上等价，并给出了虚拟腿任务空间到关节空间的力矩映射关系。

    控制方面，提出了一套面向嵌入式实现的 LQR-VMC 分层控制系统：LQR 负责机体平衡、速度跟踪与腿摆控制量分配，VMC 负责腿长方向支撑，并通过基于滚转角的腿长差修正改善侧向姿态。仿真和样机实验覆盖了原地平衡、速度跟踪、扰动恢复、腿长调节和滚转稳定等典型工况。实验表明，所提混合式结构方案和分层控制策略可在物理样机上稳定运行，验证了方法的可行性与工程实用性。
}{双轮足机器人，混合式机构，运动学与动力学建模，LQR-VMC，分层轻量级控制}

\MakeAbstractEng{
    Wheeled-biped robots combine wheeled locomotion efficiency with legged terrain adaptability. Existing wheel-legged platforms, however, often employ intricate mechanisms, numerous actuators, or computationally demanding controllers, impeding compact integration and embedded real-time operation. This thesis addresses these limitations through the structural design, dynamic modeling, and lightweight control of a hybrid wheeled-biped robot, targeting stable balance, velocity regulation, and leg-length support.

    The configuration, closed-chain leg mechanism, body structure, chain transmission, and foot wheel modules are designed, with joint and hub motors selected and validated. The closed-chain leg is abstracted as a telescopic virtual leg for controller synthesis. A planar kinematic and dynamic model is established, from which a linear state-space formulation for LQR is derived. The kinematic equivalence between the hybrid closed-chain leg and a standard five-bar linkage is established, and the torque mapping from virtual-leg task space to joint space is derived for virtual model control (VMC).

    A hierarchical LQR-VMC architecture is proposed for real-time embedded deployment: LQR governs body balancing, velocity tracking, and leg-swing torque distribution, while VMC provides leg-length support. A roll-angle feedback term modulates the leg-length differential to enhance lateral stability. Simulations and prototype experiments span standing balance, velocity tracking, disturbance rejection, leg-length variation, and roll stabilization. Experimental results confirm stable operation on the physical prototype, validating the effectiveness and engineering practicality of the proposed approach.
}{wheeled-biped robot, hybrid mechanism, dynamic modeling, LQR-VMC, lightweight control}
