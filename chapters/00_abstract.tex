\MakeAbstract{
    双轮足机器人兼具轮式移动效率和腿式机构地形适应能力，但现有轮腿平台往往存在机械结构复杂、执行器数量多和控制计算量较大的问题，限制了小型样机的集成和嵌入式实时控制。针对上述问题，本文以混合式双轮足机器人为研究对象，围绕结构设计、动力学建模和轻量级控制方法开展研究，目标是在较低控制复杂度下实现稳定平衡、速度调节和腿长支撑。

    在机械结构方面，本文完成了机器人总体构型、闭环腿部机构、躯干承载结构、链传动系统和足端轮组系统设计，并对腿部关节电机和轮毂电机进行了选型与校核。为支撑控制器设计，本文将闭环腿部机构等效为可伸缩虚拟腿，建立了机器人平面等效运动学与动力学模型，推导了面向 LQR 控制器的线性状态空间表达；同时，证明了混合式闭环腿部机构与标准五连杆机构在运动学上的等效关系，并建立了虚拟腿任务空间到关节空间的力矩映射。

    在控制方法方面，本文提出了一种面向嵌入式实时实现的 LQR-VMC 分层控制系统。该系统采用 LQR 完成机体平衡、速度跟踪和腿摆控制量分配，采用 VMC 实现腿长方向支撑，并通过基于滚转角的腿长差修正改善侧向姿态。仿真和样机实验围绕原地平衡、速度跟踪、扰动恢复、腿长调节和滚转稳定等工况展开。结果表明，所提出的混合式机械结构和分层轻量级控制方法能够在物理样机上实现稳定运行，验证了本文方法的有效性和工程可实现性。
}{双轮足机器人，混合式机构，运动学与动力学建模，LQR-VMC，分层轻量级控制}

\MakeAbstractEng{
    Wheeled-biped robots can move efficiently on wheels while using their leg mechanisms to adapt to uneven ground. Many existing wheel-legged platforms, however, use complicated structures, multiple actuators, or controllers with high computational cost, which makes compact prototyping and embedded real-time control difficult. This thesis focuses on a hybrid wheeled-biped robot and studies its mechanical design, dynamic modeling, and lightweight control.

    In the mechanical design, the overall robot configuration, closed-chain leg mechanism, body structure, chain transmission, and wheel modules are designed, and the main joint motors and hub motors are selected and checked. To support controller design, the closed-chain leg is modeled as a telescopic virtual leg. A planar kinematic and dynamic model is built, a linear state-space model for LQR control is derived, and the kinematic equivalence between the hybrid closed-chain leg and a standard five-bar linkage is analyzed. The torque mapping from the virtual-leg task space to the joint space is also obtained for VMC.

    For control, this thesis proposes a layered LQR-VMC system for embedded real-time implementation. The LQR part handles body balance, velocity tracking, and leg-swing torque allocation, while VMC provides support along the leg-length direction. A roll-angle feedback term adjusts the leg-length difference to improve lateral attitude stability. Simulations and prototype experiments cover standing balance, velocity tracking, disturbance recovery, leg-length adjustment, and roll stabilization. The results show that the mechanical structure and controller can run stably on the physical prototype with relatively low control complexity, which supports the practical feasibility of the proposed method.
}{wheeled-biped robot, hybrid mechanism, dynamic modeling, LQR-VMC, lightweight control}
